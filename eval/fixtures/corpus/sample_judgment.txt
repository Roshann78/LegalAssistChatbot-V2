IN THE SUPREME COURT OF SAMPLELAND
Sample Citizen v. State of Samples

1. This appeal concerns the right of an arrested person to be informed of the grounds of arrest.

2. The question for determination is whether failure to communicate the grounds of arrest in writing renders the arrest unlawful.

3. We hold that the grounds of arrest must be communicated to the arrested person in a language the person understands, and failure to do so vitiates the arrest.

4. The appeal is allowed.
